## Risk Assessment

### Low Risk
- ✅ Grain entities: High ROI, clear names, easy matching
- ✅ Mining giants: Global leaders, high tonnage, recognizable
- ✅ Automotive: Brand names are unique and safe

### Medium Risk
- ⚠️ Chemicals: Diverse products, some generic terms (need careful keywords)
- ⚠️ Steel: Many processors/traders, need to focus on producers

### High Risk
- 🚨 Generic names: GENERAL, UNITED STATES, CLEVELAND (require Contains_All)
- 🚨 Short names: BHP (3 letters - check for false positives)

### Mitigation
- Use Contains_All for all high-risk names
- Add industry context tokens (GENERAL MILLS, UNITED STATES STEEL)
- Manual validation of top 100 matches per new entity
- Spot-check automotive brands (common words: FORD, LAND ROVER)
