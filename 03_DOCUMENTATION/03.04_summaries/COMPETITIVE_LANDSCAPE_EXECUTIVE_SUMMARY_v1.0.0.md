- **5,631 shipper competition pairs** (entities competing in same trade lanes)

- **15 major commodities** analyzed (>100K tons each)
- **22 major ports** profiled (>5M tons each)
- **Minimum overlap threshold:** 5% market overlap, 10,000 tons minimum

## Strategic Competitive Patterns

### Pattern 1: PMI Trading Internal Competition
**Finding:** PMI Trading operates through 4+ legal entities that compete with EACH OTHER:
- PMI Trading Ltd.
- PMI Commercio Internacional SA
- PMI Trading Designated Activity Co.
- PMI Come Int SA de CO.

**Impact:** 1.0M - 1.8M tons overlap between PMI entities on same Mexico → US Gulf routes.

**Interpretation:** Legal/tax optimization strategy creating artificial competition, or separate regional divisions operating independently.

---

### Pattern 2: Middle East Crude Oil Convergence
**Finding:** Iraq SOMO, Abu Dhabi National, and Chevron Marine all compete on **identical route:**
- Origin: High Seas, North Pacific
- Destination: San Francisco
- Commodity: Crude Oil

**Overlap Matrix:**
- SOMO vs Chevron: 4.8M tons (82% of Chevron's volume)
- Abu Dhabi vs SOMO: 2.0M tons (85% of Abu Dhabi's volume)
- Abu Dhabi vs Chevron: 2.0M tons (84% of Abu Dhabi's volume)

**Interpretation:** Chevron's San Francisco refinery sources from multiple Middle Eastern suppliers, creating intense supplier competition for same buyer.

---

### Pattern 3: Venezuela Oil Fragmentation
**Finding:** Venezuelan oil exports controlled by 2 competing entities:
- PDVSA Petroleo (national oil company)
- Petropiar (joint venture)

**Overlap:** 3.4M tons (60% of Petropiar's volume, 45% of PDVSA's volume)

**Routes:** 10 overlapping trade lanes, primarily Venezuela → Mobile

**Interpretation:** Internal competition within Venezuelan oil industry, possibly reflecting different upstream asset ownership or legacy contract structures.

---

### Pattern 4: UAE Aluminum Duopoly
**Finding:** Two UAE aluminum smelters compete head-to-head:
- Dubai Aluminium (Dubal)
- Emirates Aluminium (EGA)

**Overlap:** 3.1M tons across 16 trade lanes (79% of Dubal, 74% of EGA)

**Primary route:** UAE → Baltimore (general cargo classification)

**Market structure:** Near-perfect duopoly with minimal product differentiation, competing for same US buyers on identical routes.

---

### Pattern 5: Aggregates Vertical Integration vs. Competition
**Finding:** Martin Marietta exhibits dual strategy:
- **Internal sourcing:** Imports 5.3M tons from own Canadian subsidiary
- **External sourcing:** Competes with Orca Sand and Gravel (5.6M tons) on same Nova Scotia → Florida routes

**Interpretation:** Martin Marietta supplements own production with competitor purchases when demand exceeds internal capacity, or sources specific grades not produced internally.

## Methodology Notes

**Trade Lane Definition:**
```
Origin_Port → Destination_Port | Commodity
```

**Overlap Calculation:**
- For each entity pair, identify overlapping trade lanes
- Overlap tonnage = MIN(Entity1_tons, Entity2_tons) per lane
- Overlap % = Overlap / Total entity tonnage

**Filtering Thresholds:**
- Minimum entity tonnage: 10,000 tons (exclude insignificant players)
- Minimum overlap %: 5% (exclude trivial overlaps)
- Port analysis: >5M tons total (major ports only)
- Commodity analysis: >100K tons total (significant commodities)

**Performance:**
- **Shipper pairs analyzed:** 1.6M combinations
- **Consignee pairs analyzed:** 1.8M combinations
- **Runtime:** ~10 minutes
- **Memory usage:** ~4 GB peak
