ENTITY NAMING CONVENTIONS
================================================================================

Entity_ID Format: [COMPANY]-[VARIANT]-[NUMBER]
  Examples:
    - SHELL-BRASIL-001 (geographic variant)
    - HYUNDAI-MOTOR-001 (business unit variant)
    - MORTON-BAHAMAS-001 (location variant)

Canonical_Name: Official company name
  - Use full legal name where appropriate
  - Include "USA", "Inc", "Corp" for US subsidiaries
  - Use "of America" for US operations of foreign companies

Parent_Company: Ultimate parent or controlling entity
  - Leave blank for independent companies
  - Use for tracking ownership relationships

Match_Keywords: Semicolon-separated patterns
  - Order by specificity (most specific first)
  - Include common variations and typos
  - Avoid overly broad patterns
