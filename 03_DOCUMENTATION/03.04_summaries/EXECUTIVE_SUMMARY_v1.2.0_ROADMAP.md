## Key Advantages of Multi-Field Entities

**Multi-field entities = Higher ROI**

| Entity Type | Entities | Avg Tons/Entity | Fields Matched | ROI Factor |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|----------------|------------|
| Single-field | 18 | 2.1M | 1 | 1.0x |
| Multi-field | 22 | 1.8M | 2-3 | 2.5x |

**Why Multi-Field Matters:**
- One dictionary entry matches 2-3 fields per shipment
- Reduces maintenance burden (fewer entries to update)
- Higher confidence in matching (cross-field validation)
- Better entity resolution (same company appears consistently)

**Recommendation:** Prioritize multi-field entities in Top 40
