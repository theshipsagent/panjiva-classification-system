TOP 10 DISCOVERIES
================================================================================

1. IRVING OIL'S UNIQUE STRATEGY
   - ONLY major refiner using 100% small tankers (Handy/MR 25-60K DWT)
   - 84% carrier concentration with Vroon B.V. (6 vessels)
   - Suggests coastal distribution vs offshore deep-water strategy
   - 15.9M tons imported (87% of their total tonnage)

2. SAUDI REFINING'S EXCLUSIVE PARTNERSHIP
   - 99% of tonnage moves via AET Inc Pte Ltd
   - 100% Aframax tankers (60-120K DWT)
   - 213 shipments, 12 vessels
   - 15.0M tons - most standardized operation in dataset

3. CHEVRON'S CAPTIVE FLEET
   - Largest self-operated fleet: 21.9M tons (37% of Chevron's total)
   - 22 vessels under Chevron Shipping Company
   - Three dedicated shuttle vessels: APOLLO/POLARIS/PEGASUS VOYAGER
   - 73, 61, and 59 visits respectively = weekly crude imports

4. VALERO'S SPOT MARKET STRATEGY
   - Most diversified carrier portfolio
   - Largest carrier = only 24% (N Two - 10.4M tons)
   - Uses 3+ major carriers: N Two (24%), Penfield (19%), AET (14%)
   - No single dominant partnership = spot market trading

5. TERNIUM'S STEEL SHUTTLE SERVICE
   - MINANUR CEBI 1: 88 visits in 2024 (most frequent vessel-entity pair)
   - Weekly steel imports from single vessel
   - 36K tons total (low tonnage, high frequency)
   - Suggests just-in-time manufacturing supply chain

6. MARTIN MARIETTA'S GREAT LAKES MONOPOLY
   - 100% carrier concentration: CSL Group Inc
   - 8.5M tons aggregates (100% of entity tonnage)
   - 15 vessels, zero shipment records (data artifact)
   - CSL = dominant Great Lakes bulk carrier

7. EMIRATES ALUMINIUM'S DUAL MODE
   - 69% bulk carriers (10.6M tons bauxite)
   - 51% vehicle carriers BY SHIPMENTS (356 shipments, only 6K tons)
   - Enesel Bulk: 52% concentration for bulk bauxite
   - Hyundai Glovis: vehicle carriers for finished aluminum products

8. CARRIER SPECIALIZATION PHENOMENON
   - 813 carriers with 90%+ concentration in single vessel type
   - Stolt Tankers: 100% chemical tankers (75 vessels)
   - Hyundai Glovis: 96% vehicle carriers (112 vessels)
   - AET Inc: 100% tankers (25 vessels, 33M tons)

9. RECURRING FLEET RELATIONSHIPS
   - 267 vessel-entity pairs with 3+ visits in 2024
   - Top 10 recurring vessels: 30-88 visits per year
   - Chevron's -VOYAGER fleet: 3 vessels, 60+ visits each
   - Suggests dedicated time charter contracts vs spot market

10. VESSEL SIZE STRATEGY PATTERNS
    - VLCC (200-320K DWT) = Marathon (35%), long-haul Middle East
    - Suezmax (120-200K DWT) = Chevron (43%), balanced crude
    - Aframax (60-120K DWT) = Standard size (most common)
    - Handy/MR (25-60K DWT) = Irving Oil (87%), coastal only
    - Size correlates with trade lane + port draft limitations

BUSINESS INSIGHTS
================================================================================

DEDICATED FLEETS vs SPOT MARKET:
- >80% carrier concentration = contract-of-affreightment (COA)
- <30% concentration = spot market chartering
- Irving Oil + Saudi Refining = most dedicated (84%, 99%)
- Valero + ExxonMobil = most flexible (24%, 25% max)

VESSEL SIZE STRATEGY:
- Port draft limitations drive vessel size choices
- Offshore terminals accept VLCC/Suezmax (Marathon, Chevron)
- Coastal refineries limited to Handy/MR (Irving Oil)
- Aframax = "goldilocks" size (most common for crude imports)

CARRIER SPECIALIZATION:
- 813 carriers with 90%+ single vessel type
- Stolt = chemical tankers (75 vessels)
- Hyundai Glovis = vehicle carriers (112 vessels)
- Niche specialists dominate certain trades

SUPPLY CHAIN PATTERNS:
- Chevron: Owned fleet + chartered supplements (37% + 18%)
- Ternium: Weekly steel shuttles (88 visits from single vessel)
- Emirates Aluminium: Bulk bauxite + vehicle carrier for products
- Vulcan Materials: Coastal barge network (2 barges, 76 visits total)
