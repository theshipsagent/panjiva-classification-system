## Executive Summary

This analysis reveals true market concentration by aggregating subsidiary tonnage to parent company level. While entity-level analysis shows 22 distinct shippers and 21 consignees, consolidation to parent companies reduces this to just **21 parent companies controlling 228M tons (shipper side) and 268M tons (consignee side)**.

**Key Finding:** Corporate families like **PBF Energy** (3 subsidiaries), **Emirates Global Aluminium** (2 subsidiaries), and **Saudi Aramco** (2 subsidiaries) increase their market share significantly when consolidated.

## Methodology Notes

**Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI):**
- Formula: Sum of squared market shares (in percentage points)
- Range: 0-10,000
- <1,500: Competitive
- 1,500-2,500: Moderate concentration
- >2,500: Highly concentrated

**CR4/CR8 (Concentration Ratios):**
- CR4: Market share of top 4 firms
- CR8: Market share of top 8 firms
- Higher values = more concentrated market

**Parent Company Mapping:**
- If `Parent_Company` field exists in dictionary → use parent
- If `Parent_Company` is null → entity is top-level (use canonical name)
- Subsidiaries rolled up to parent for all tonnage calculations
